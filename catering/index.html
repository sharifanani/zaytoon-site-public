FAQs

Everything you need to know.

What is the minimum order for catering?

We require a minimum of 15 people for Build Your Bowl buffets. Individual boxed lunches are available for any quantity, making them perfect for smaller meetings or drop-offs.

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend ordering 24–48 hours in advance for best selection and timing. We frequently accommodate same-day requests when possible — call (614) 363-4131 to check same-day availability.

Do you provide setup and service?

Yes! Every buffet order includes complete professional setup at no additional charge. Our delivery team arranges everything and provides serving utensils. Chafer stands and burners are available as an optional add-on if you’d like hot-holding equipment. For boxed lunches, we deliver and arrange individual boxes for easy distribution.

Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. The Build Your Bowl format makes it easy — guests create their own bowls with exactly what they want. We clearly label all items, including vegetarian, vegan and gluten-friendly options. For specific allergies, let us know when ordering and we’ll provide detailed ingredient information.

Is your meat halal certified?

Yes! All our chicken and beef are 100% halal certified. We’re proud to serve Columbus’s Muslim community and anyone seeking halal options. Certification information is available on request.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and cash. For corporate accounts we can set up invoicing and accept purchase orders — email info@zaytoonrestaurant.com to set up an account with priority scheduling and flexible payment.

What areas do you deliver to?

We deliver throughout the Columbus metro area, typically within 40 minutes of our location (up to 35 miles). Online checkout will confirm whether your address is in our delivery zone. Questions? Call (614) 363-4131.
