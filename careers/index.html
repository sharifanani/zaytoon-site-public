Quick answers

Working at Zaytoon.

Do I need experience?

Nope. We train you from day one. Some of our best people had zero restaurant experience.

How much are tips?

Everyone shares tips. The amount is added on top of your base pay every shift. Ask us about the rate for your location when you message us.

Can I work around school?

Yes. A lot of our team are students. Give us your availability and we’ll make a schedule that works for you.

My English is OK but not perfect. Can I still apply?

If you can take a customer’s order and answer basic menu questions in English, that’s enough. Speaking two languages is a bonus here.

What’s Zaytoon?

Build-your-own Mediterranean bowls and pitas. Halal, made from scratch every day. Think Chipotle, but Mediterranean.

Don’t see your role?

Message us anyway. Tell us about you.

We’re always happy to meet good people.
