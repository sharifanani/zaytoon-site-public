zay·toon زيتون
/zay-TOON/  ·  noun, Arabic
  1. The olive — fruit of the tree that has fed the Mediterranean for thousands of years.
  2. A symbol of peace, generosity, and a table that always has room for one more.
  3. A Mediterranean grill in Columbus, Ohio, where good food brings people together.
